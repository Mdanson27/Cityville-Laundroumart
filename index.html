About CityVille

Freshness, without the wait.

CityVille Laundromat is built around one simple promise: make professional laundry easier, faster and more convenient for people in Kampala.

From washing and controlled drying to careful folding, garments move through a professional process designed to return them clean, fresh and ready to use. CityVille uses professional LG commercial washing and drying equipment.

24hour freshness mindset

Drop off in the morning and enjoy professionally cleaned, folded laundry by the end of the day on same-day eligible loads.
